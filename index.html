Our Craft

The Art of
Creation

01

Yarn & Zari Selection

Every saree begins with pure mulberry silk yarn and genuine zari — silver thread dipped in gold — sourced the way Kanchipuram weavers have for generations.

02

Korvai Handloom Weaving

The body and contrast border are woven separately and interlocked by hand on the loom — the ancient Korvai technique that gives a genuine Kanjivaram its telltale reversible border.

03

Pallu & Border Finishing

The pallu is woven last and finished by hand — checked for weave density, motif alignment, and zari purity before it ever reaches your hands.
